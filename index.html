How it works

If Arsenal hit the post twice and Chelsea once, Arsenal win the match 2–1 and take three points — whatever the actual football score was.

Teams level on points are separated by woodwork difference, then woodwork for, then alphabetically.

Data is pulled from the open-source FPL Core Insights dataset, which is refreshed during the season.
